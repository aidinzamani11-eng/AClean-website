Who We're Looking For

Do You Have What It Takes?

You don't need years of experience — we'll train the right person. What you do need is the right attitude. Here's what matters most.

🪜

Comfortable At Heights

Much of our work happens on ladders, roofs, and water-fed poles two to four storeys up. You need to be steady, safety-conscious, and at ease working off the ground.

💪

Willing To Work Hard

This is physical, outdoor work in all weather. We're looking for people who show up, push through, and take real pride in leaving every property spotless.

⏰

Reliable & On Time

Our customers count on us to show up when we say we will. If you're dependable and treat people's homes with respect, you'll fit right in.

🚗

Valid License & Transport

A valid driver's license and reliable transportation to get to job sites are a strong asset. We travel across the Fraser Valley daily.

🤝

Great With People

You'll be the face of AClean on every job. Friendly, professional, and respectful goes a long way with our residential and commercial clients.

🌱

Eager To Learn

From pure-water systems to soft washing, we use professional-grade equipment and methods. Come ready to learn the trade the right way.
