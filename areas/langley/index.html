We Know Langley

Langley is AClean's home territory. We've cleaned properties across Langley City, Langley Township, and every neighbourhood in between — from the dense suburban streets of Willowbrook and Murrayville to the acreage properties of Fort Langley and Brookswood. This is where we do most of our work and where we're fastest to respond.

Langley's wet climate, significant tree canopy, and mix of property types create unique exterior cleaning demands. Roof moss is a persistent issue across the Township — the shaded areas of Walnut Grove and Aldergrove see particularly aggressive lichen and algae growth that can shorten roof life by 5–10 years if left untreated. We use a professional-grade soft wash process that kills organisms at the root without damaging your shingles or voiding your roofing warranty.

Window cleaning in Langley is a year-round need — the combination of hard water minerals, pollen from Langley's many trees, and road spray from Highway 1 means windows that cleaned in spring will be visibly dirty again by fall. We recommend 2–4 cleans per year depending on your property's exposure.

Langley has everything from compact townhomes in Willowbrook to large acreage estates in Fort Langley. We bring the right equipment for every property type — including water-fed poles for multi-storey homes and extended reach for large rural lots.

Langley Township's mature tree canopy is beautiful — and brutal on gutters. Walnut Grove, Aldergrove, and Brookswood properties typically need two gutter cleans per year: one after spring leaf-out and one after the fall drop.
