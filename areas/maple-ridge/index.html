Our Services in
Maple Ridge BC

From forested acreages to suburban strata — our Maple Ridge exterior cleaning services are tailored to the unique demands of this community.

🪟
Window Cleaning

Streak-free window cleaning for Maple Ridge homes and commercial properties. Interior and exterior using pure water and traditional methods on all property sizes.

 🍃
Gutter Cleaning

Maple Ridge's forest surroundings fill gutters fast. We clear, flush, and inspect gutters to prevent overflow, fascia damage, and foundation erosion — twice yearly recommended.

 🏠
Roof Washing

Soft wash roof cleaning for asphalt shingles and cedar shakes. Low-pressure technique removes moss, algae and lichen without damaging your roofing material.

 ⚡
Pressure Washing

High-power pressure washing for Maple Ridge driveways, patios, walkways and parking areas. Restore concrete and paving to near-new condition quickly and effectively.

 🏡
House Washing

Full exterior house washing removing mould, algae, and grime from all siding types across Maple Ridge — vinyl, hardie board, wood, stucco, and brick.

 🌊
Soft Washing

Gentle low-pressure washing for cedar shake roofs, painted wood, fences, and older Maple Ridge homes that require a delicate touch over raw pressure.
