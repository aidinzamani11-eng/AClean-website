Why Abbotsford Homes Need Professional Cleaning

Abbotsford's Wet Winters
Create Year-Round
Cleaning Challenges

Abbotsford receives over 1,500 mm of rain annually — placing it consistently among BC's wettest cities. The combination of wet winters and warm, humid summers creates ideal conditions for moss and algae growth on rooftops, siding, and concrete surfaces across the city.

From the new subdivisions of East Abbotsford to the established neighbourhoods of Clearbrook and West Abbotsford, virtually every home in the city deals with green algae on north-facing walls and roof sections. Properties near Sumas Prairie and rural areas east of town often face even heavier buildup due to increased moisture and agricultural dust settling on surfaces.

AClean serves Abbotsford regularly and understands the specific cladding types common here — stucco, Hardie board, and vinyl siding each require different cleaning approaches. We tailor our process to your property so you get lasting results without damage to painted or delicate surfaces.

🌧️

1,500mm+ Annual Rainfall

Abbotsford is one of BC's wettest cities. This constant moisture promotes moss on rooftops and algae on all north-facing surfaces year-round — making annual professional cleaning essential for property maintenance.

🏘️

Dense New Development

Abbotsford's rapid growth means thousands of homes built in the last decade — many with stucco and Hardie board that's highly susceptible to green staining. Soft washing is the only safe method for these surfaces.

🌾

Agricultural Dust & Pollen

Abbotsford's farming community means high pollen and agricultural dust loads in spring and summer. This settles on windows and siding, dulling glass and staining painted surfaces — particularly for homes near Sumas Prairie and the rural east.
