Why It Matters

More Than Just Clean Screens

01
Improved Ventilation

Clogged screens restrict airflow by up to 50% in peak pollen season. Clean screens let fresh air flow freely through your home without obstruction.

02
Remove Allergens

Pollen, mold spores, and dust embedded in screen mesh blow through into your living space every time a breeze passes. Regular cleaning removes the allergen source entirely.

03
Restore Clear Views

Built-up grime on screens creates a grey film across your view even when the glass itself is clean. Freshly washed screens restore a clear, unobstructed outlook from every window.

04
Extend Screen Life

Pollen and oxidation degrade screen mesh and frames over time. Regular cleaning removes corrosive buildup, significantly extending the life of your screens.
