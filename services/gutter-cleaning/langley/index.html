Why Langley Gutters Need Attention

Blocked Gutters Cost
Langley Homeowners
Thousands Every Year

Langley is AClean's home base — and after hundreds of properties serviced across Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Aldergrove, and Fort Langley, we know exactly what blocks gutters here. The combination of towering maple and cedar trees, Fraser Valley rainfall patterns averaging 1,100mm annually, and large acreage lots with surrounding vegetation means gutters in Langley fill fast — often completely blocking in a single season.

When gutters back up, water doesn't just overflow — it infiltrates. Fascia boards rot from the inside out, soffits develop mould, foundation waterproofing is overwhelmed, and basements flood. A $150–$200 gutter clean prevents a $5,000–$20,000 repair bill. It is the single highest-ROI maintenance task a Langley homeowner can perform each year.

Our process goes beyond scooping debris. We hand-clear the trough, wet-vac stubborn packed matter, flush every section with water, and test every downspout for clear flow from top to outlet. We also report on any visible gutter damage — loose spikes, sagging sections, failing end caps — so you can address issues before they worsen.
