Langley is AClean's home base — we clean more windows here than anywhere else in the Fraser Valley, and we know the area's property types, water quality, and seasonal patterns intimately. Langley's water is moderately hard, which means mineral deposits accumulate on exterior glass faster than homeowners expect. The tree canopy across Walnut Grove, Aldergrove, and Brookswood deposits pollen and organic matter on windows throughout spring and fall.

We use pure water reach-and-wash technology for exterior upper-storey windows — a water-fed pole system using water filtered to near-zero TDS, which leaves zero mineral spots when it dries. For ground-floor and interior glass, we use traditional squeegee and microfibre for a hand-finished result.

We serve Langley City, Langley Township, and all rural acreage properties — no travel surcharge for any Langley address. Most bookings are completed within 3–5 business days of enquiry.
