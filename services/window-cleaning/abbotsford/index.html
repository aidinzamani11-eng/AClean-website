Deserve Spotless
Windows

Abbotsford receives over 1,500 mm of rain annually, and that rain — combined with the city's hard water mineral content — leaves a hazy film on exterior windows that builds up month over month. Add spring pollen from the surrounding agricultural valley and summer dust from Sumas Prairie, and Abbotsford windows get dirty faster than almost anywhere else in the Lower Mainland.

We use pure water technology — water filtered through reverse osmosis to near-zero TDS — which evaporates without leaving any mineral spots or streaks. Our water-fed pole systems reach 3–4 storeys safely from the ground, meaning no ladders on your roof or risk of damage to gutters.

For interior glass, we use professional squeegee and microfibre technique for a hand-finished result. Every service includes frames, sills, and tracks — and we wear protective footwear inside your home.
