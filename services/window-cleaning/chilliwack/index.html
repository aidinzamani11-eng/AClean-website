Professional Window Cleaning in Chilliwack

Why Chilliwack Windows
Get Dirty So Fast

Chilliwack sits at the convergence of the Fraser Valley and the mountain climate, which means windows here face a unique combination of stresses. Heavy rainfall deposits hard water minerals on exterior glass. The region's long pollen season — driven by the surrounding mountains and valley farmland — coats windows in a fine yellow film each spring. And properties near the Vedder Road and Highway 1 corridors collect road grime and diesel exhaust that bonds to glass over time.

We use two methods depending on your property and window type. Our pure water reach-and-wash system uses water filtered to near-zero TDS through reverse osmosis — allowing us to clean second and third-storey windows safely from the ground, leaving zero mineral spots or streaks when the water evaporates. For ground-floor and interior windows, we use traditional squeegee and microfibre technique for a perfect hand-finished result.

Every window clean includes frames, sills, and visible tracks at no extra charge. We communicate before arrival, follow up on completion, and won't leave until you're satisfied.
