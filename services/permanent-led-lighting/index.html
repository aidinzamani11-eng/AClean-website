Common Questions

LED Lighting FAQs

Yes. Permanent LED lighting is professionally installed with concealed mounting channels, weatherproof connectors, and integrated wiring — designed to remain on your home year-round without seasonal removal. The LEDs themselves are rated for 50,000+ hours of operation.

The system is controlled via a dedicated smartphone app (iOS and Android). You can select any colour, brightness, and animation pattern. Pre-programmed holiday themes are available for Christmas, Halloween, Canada Day, and more — one tap to change your display.

Our installation team mounts LED track channels along your roofline, gables, and architectural features. Wiring is concealed as much as possible and connected to a dedicated outdoor power supply. A typical residential installation takes 1–2 days.

Permanent LED systems are extremely energy-efficient. A typical residential roofline installation (approx. 100–200 ft) consumes less electricity than a few standard light bulbs when running at full brightness — typically under $10–$20/month depending on usage.

Individual LED sections can be replaced without rewiring the entire system. We offer a warranty on parts and labour, and annual maintenance inspections to ensure your system continues to perform reliably year-round.
