Yes. We use low-pressure soft washing with biodegradable solutions formulated specifically for asphalt shingles, cedar shakes, and tile roofs. High-pressure washing is never used on roofing — it can void manufacturer warranties and cause irreversible damage.

In the Fraser Valley, we recommend cleaning gutters at least twice per year — once in late spring after pollen season and once in late fall after the leaves drop. Properties surrounded by trees may need quarterly cleaning to prevent blockages, water overflow, and foundation damage.
